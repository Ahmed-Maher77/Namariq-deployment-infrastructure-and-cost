How the numbers are worked out

Prices come from Oracle's public price list, plus fixed usage assumptions for backups, files, traffic and e-mail.

Show the assumptions and sources
  • Backups: a backup is half the database size; 2% of the data changes a day.
  • Each client a month: 2,000 e-mails, 50 GB of traffic, 0.5 GB of logs.
  • Free allowances (such as the first 10 GB) count once per Oracle account.
  • VAT is added to every price. Check with your accountant whether it applies to your Oracle bill.
  • User capacity is an estimate. Test it with ops/loadtest.py before promising it to a client.
  • Sources: Oracle price list API, Oracle price list, Oracle Always Free resources, Oracle cost estimator.
