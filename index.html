How the numbers are worked out

  • Oracle charges the same price in every commercial region and quotes prices before tax. The VAT you choose is added to everything, Oracle and other providers. Check with your accountant whether VAT applies to your Oracle invoice; it depends on the billing country and VAT registration.
  • Free allowances (Always Free, the first 10 GB, the first 3,000 e-mails) apply once per Oracle account. The calculator adds up every client's usage, applies the free allowance once, and spreads the result over the parts that use that service. A client with their own Oracle account gets their own free allowances.
  • Always Free Arm server: Oracle's Always Free page says 1,500 OCPU-hours and 9,000 GB-hours a month (2 OCPU, 12 GB). The price list still shows 3,000 and 18,000. This page uses the lower number.
  • Always Free servers exist only in the account's home region, and Arm capacity is sometimes unavailable for a while. Keep the paid ClamAV fallback in mind.
  • User and request capacity are planning estimates for this Django app, about 50 active users per OCPU on the app server. Confirm them with ops/loadtest.py before promising numbers to a client.
  • Domain and e-mail prices are public list prices in USD before tax, checked 2026-11-23. Promotions and local prices differ.
  • Sources: Oracle price list API, Oracle price list, Oracle Always Free resources, Oracle cost estimator.
