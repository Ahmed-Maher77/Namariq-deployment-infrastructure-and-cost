Notes and sources

  • Oracle charges the same price in every commercial region. Oracle prices exclude tax; the VAT you choose is added on top of everything (Oracle and other providers). Confirm with your accountant whether VAT applies to your Oracle invoice (it depends on the billing country and VAT registration).
  • Free allowances (Always Free, first 10 GB, first 3,000 e-mails…) apply once per Oracle account. The calculator adds up every client's usage, applies the free tiers once, and spreads the result over the parts that use that Oracle service. A client with their own Oracle account gets their own free allowances.
  • Always Free Arm (A1): Oracle's Always Free page says 1,500 OCPU-hours and 9,000 GB-hours per month (2 OCPU + 12 GB). The price list still shows 3,000 / 18,000 free. This page uses the lower, safer number.
  • Always Free compute exists only in the account's home region and Arm capacity is sometimes temporarily unavailable ("out of host capacity"). Keep the paid ClamAV fallback in mind.
  • User and request capacity figures are planning estimates for this Django app (about 50 active users per OCPU on the app server). Confirm them with ops/loadtest.py before promising numbers to a client.
  • Domain and e-mail prices are public list prices in USD before tax, checked 2026-11-23; promotions and local prices differ.
  • Sources: Oracle price list API · Oracle price list · Oracle Always Free resources · Oracle cost estimator
